What to include

For a library behavior report, include the version you are using, the smallest relevant form markup, the options passed to JnFormValidation, the steps to reproduce, and what you expected to happen. Remove passwords, personal data, and other secrets from examples.

For setup questions, see the installation guide, options reference, and FAQ first.
